How the score is built

Every source is normalised to a 0–1 signal, then:

score = 100 × max(
    Σ(weight × signal) / Σ(weight),   ← weighted mean of the sources
                                         that actually answered
    max(signal × authority)           ← authority floor: one confirmed
)                                        C2 listing still reads critical

Then the modifiers an analyst would apply by hand: GreyNoise benign damps the score ×0.45, an allowlist entry forces 0, a blocklist entry forces ≥ 90. Confidence is reported separately — 85/100 from one source is not 85/100 from five. Open any indicator and press Scoring math to see the arithmetic.
