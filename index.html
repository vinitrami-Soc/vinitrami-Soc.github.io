CASE-006 // SOC AUTOMATION✓ OPEN SOURCE

PhishHawk: Automated Phishing IOC & Threat Triage Engine

A terminal tool that runs the first ten minutes of L1 phishing triage by itself. It unwraps reported mail and catches lookalikes of your own domain even when SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. It also sees through Safe Links, redirects and HTML smuggling, and enriches via VirusTotal, URLScan.io, RDAP and AbuseIPDB. Verdicts are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Evaluated on 400 real honeypot phishing emails: 71% caught on a held-out sample, with no API keys.
